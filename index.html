This is a practice and training aid. It does not diagnose neglect, ADHD, or any other condition, does not measure IQ, and is not a treatment or a substitute for assessment and therapy by a qualified clinician.

Use it to practise a scanning strategy your therapist has shown you. Your results are private practice records for this device.

New or suddenly worse symptoms? Weakness, speech changes, sudden vision loss, or a severe headache can be an emergency. Stop and seek urgent medical care now. Do not do more exercises.

Before your first session

Check with a doctor, occupational therapist, or speech-language pathologist before starting if a clinician has not discussed scanning practice with you, your symptoms are severe, or you have trouble staying safe.

Find the red line

Find the red line. Turn your head and eyes all the way to the left. Then sweep slowly to the right. Move down one row and start at the left again.

Today’s session

One short symbol round, one reading task, and one seated table search. Take a break whenever you need one.

Position: sit comfortably. Centre the screen on your body. Keep it on a table. Use either hand.

For real objects away from the screen, ask someone to stay with you. Do not stand or walk to find them.
